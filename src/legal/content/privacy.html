Privacy Policy

syncall.ai | Last updated 20 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Vornyx Media LLP ("we", "us" or "our"), the operator of syncall.ai, collects, uses, shares and protects personal data when you use the syncall.ai website and the application at app.syncall.ai (together, the "Service").

We process personal data in accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the rules made under it, as each applies.

1. Who we are

syncall.ai is a marketing analytics service operated by Vornyx Media LLP (LLPIN ACP-4541), a limited liability partnership registered in India with its registered office at New T-13/14, Uttam Nagar, Matiala, New Delhi, West Delhi 110059, Delhi, India. For the personal data of account holders described in this policy, we are the data fiduciary.

Our customers are businesses, typically marketing agencies, that bring their own clients' advertising, analytics and CRM data into syncall.ai. For that customer data, the customer decides what is uploaded or connected and why, and we process it only on the customer's instructions to provide the Service. If your personal data is contained in a customer's data, please contact that customer first; we will help them respond to you.

3. What we take from connected platforms

When you connect a platform, we request read-only access and only the permissions needed for reporting. We never post, edit, spend or send messages on your behalf.

From advertising, social and analytics platforms we store daily performance figures, such as spend, impressions, clicks, conversions, reach and followers, together with the names and identifiers of the accounts, pages, properties and campaigns they belong to.

From CRM and email platforms (HubSpot, Pipedrive, Salesforce and Mailchimp) we store totals only, such as the number of contacts created or deals won per day. We do not copy names, email addresses or other details of the individual people in those systems.

Uploaded files are read when you upload them and only the resulting figures are stored. The original file is not kept.

4. How we use personal data

  • To provide, operate and maintain the Service, including syncing and displaying the data you connect.
  • To authenticate you, keep your account secure and prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To send service emails such as email verification, password resets, security alerts, notices that a connected platform needs to be reconnected, and billing notices. These are necessary to run your account.
  • To send product updates and summaries, only if you have them switched on. You can turn them off at any time in Settings.
  • To answer support requests.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations, and to establish or defend legal claims.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use customer data to advertise to anyone, and we do not combine one customer's data with another's.

5. Consent

We process your personal data on the basis of the consent you give when you create an account, and for the legitimate uses permitted by law, such as meeting legal obligations. You may withdraw consent at any time by emailing support@syncall.ai or by closing your account. Withdrawal is as easy as giving consent. It does not affect processing already carried out, but it may mean we can no longer provide the Service to you.

6. AI features

syncall.ai includes "Ask your data", which answers questions about your organisation's metrics. When you use it, your question, the conversation so far and the relevant aggregated metrics from your organisation are sent to Anthropic, PBC, which provides the AI model that writes the answer. We send only what is needed to answer the question. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, data sent through its API is not used to train its models. If you do not use Ask your data, no data is sent to Anthropic.

AI answers can be incomplete or wrong. Check important figures against your dashboards before relying on them.

7. Data from Google and other connected platforms

When you connect a Google service, such as Google Analytics, Search Console, YouTube, Google Ads or Google Sheets, we access only the data needed for the feature you turn on, and we use it only to show that data to you inside syncall.ai. syncall.ai's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The same principle applies to every connected platform, including Meta (Facebook, Instagram and Meta Ads), LinkedIn, Pinterest, Snapchat, Amazon Ads, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Salesforce and Mailchimp: we read the data you authorise, use it only to provide the Service to you, do not sell it, do not use it for advertising and do not use it to train AI models.

You can disconnect a login at any time in Connectors. Disconnecting deletes the stored access token and stops further syncing. Figures already synced stay in your workspace until you delete them or close your account. You can also revoke our access from the platform's own account settings. See Data deletion for how to have the data itself removed.

We share personal data only with service providers that help us run the Service, under terms that require them to protect it and use it only to provide their service to us. The current list, with each provider's purpose and location, is on our Sub-processors page. We will update that page before adding a new provider that handles personal data.

We may also disclose personal data where the law requires it, in response to a valid request from a government authority or court, or to protect our rights, users or the public. If Vornyx Media LLP is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, personal data may transfer to the successor, which will remain bound by this policy.

9. Where data is stored and transfers outside India

The Service, its database, uploaded files and our connection service are hosted on a Hostinger virtual private server in India (Mumbai). We plan to move hosting to Google Cloud in India and will update this policy before we do. Some providers process data outside India: Anthropic (United States) when you use Ask your data, and our email provider when we send you email. We transfer data only to countries not restricted by the Government of India, and only to providers that protect it to the standard described in this policy.

10. How long we keep personal data

  • While your account is open, we keep your account and customer data so the Service can work.
  • Campaign-level figures from connected platforms are kept for 90 days. Daily account totals are kept while your account is open.
  • When an account is closed, we delete its personal data and customer data, and remove its connections to third-party platforms, within 30 days. Copies in backups are overwritten within a further 30 days.
  • Expired sign-in sessions are deleted automatically.
  • We keep billing and invoice records for as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires, even after an account is closed.
  • We keep security and activity records for at least 180 days, as Indian law requires, and longer only where needed to investigate fraud or abuse or where the law requires it.

11. Security

We protect personal data with reasonable security practices, described in our Security Overview, including:

  • encryption in transit (HTTPS) for all connections to the Service, and encryption at rest on our hosting provider;
  • passwords stored only as salted one-way hashes, never in readable form;
  • sign-in sessions held in secure, HTTP-only cookies, and account lockout after repeated failed sign-in attempts;
  • read-only, least-privilege permissions on connected platforms, with access tokens held by our self-hosted connection service rather than a third-party aggregator;
  • checks on every request that keep each organisation's data separate, and admin-only controls for connecting platforms and managing clients;
  • access to production systems limited to the people who need it.

No system is completely secure. Keep your password private and notify us promptly if you think your account has been misused.

12. Data breaches

If a breach affects your personal data, we will tell you without undue delay, describing what happened, the likely impact and what we are doing about it. We will also report the breach to the Data Protection Board of India, CERT-In and other authorities as the law requires.

14. Cookies and local storage

The syncall.ai application uses one strictly necessary cookie to keep you signed in. It cannot be switched off, because the Service does not work without it. We also store a few preferences, such as your light or dark theme, in your browser's local storage.

We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies in the application.

15. Children
