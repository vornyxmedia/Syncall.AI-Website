Data Deletion Instructions

syncall.ai | Last updated 19 October 2026

This page explains how to remove the data syncall.ai holds about you or about a platform account you connected, including Facebook, Instagram, Google, LinkedIn and the other platforms we support.

Remove a connected platform

  1. Sign in at app.syncall.ai as an admin of your organisation.
  2. Open Connectors and find the platform.
  3. Choose Disconnect on the login you want to remove.

Disconnecting deletes the stored access token and stops all further syncing straight away. Figures already synced from that login stay in your workspace until you ask us to delete them, as described below.

You can also remove syncall.ai from the platform's own settings. For Facebook and Instagram, go to Settings and privacy, then Settings, then Apps and websites (or Business Integrations), and remove syncall.ai. For Google, go to your Google Account, then Security, then Third-party connections.

Delete your whole account

Account owners can close their account in Settings. When the last member of an organisation leaves, the organisation and all of its data are deleted. We remove connections to third-party platforms and delete personal and customer data within 30 days. Copies in backups are overwritten within a further 30 days.

Ask us to delete specific data

Email support@syncall.ai from the email address on your account, with the subject "Data deletion request", and tell us what you want removed, for example all data synced from a particular Facebook page or ad account. We may ask you to confirm your identity. We will confirm when the deletion is complete, within 30 days of your request.

If your data is held in syncall.ai by a business that uses our Service, such as an agency that manages your advertising, please contact that business first. We will help them act on your request.

What we keep

We keep billing and invoice records for as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires, and security records for the period described in our Privacy Policy.
