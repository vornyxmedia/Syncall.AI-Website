Cancellation and Refund Policy

syncall.ai | Last updated 19 October 2026

This policy applies to paid syncall.ai subscriptions sold by Vornyx Media LLP. It is also section 9 of our Terms of Service.

Cancellation

You can cancel at any time from Settings, or by emailing support@syncall.ai. Cancellation stops the next renewal. You keep access to paid features until the end of the period you have already paid for.

Refunds

  • First payment: if you are not satisfied, you can request a full refund within seven days of your first payment for a plan by emailing support@syncall.ai.
  • After those seven days, and for all renewals, payments are not refundable, and we do not refund partly used periods.
  • Duplicate or wrongly processed charges are always refunded in full.
  • If we end your subscription for reasons that are not your fault, we will refund the unused part of your current period.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are processed within five to seven business days to the original payment method through our payment processor, Razorpay. Your bank may take additional time to credit the refund to your account.

Contact

Email support@syncall.ai with your account email and the payment reference. See Contact for our other details.
