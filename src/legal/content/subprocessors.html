Platforms you connect

The platforms you connect are not our sub-processors: you hold your own account with each of them, and we read data from them on your instructions. Supported platforms include Google (Analytics, Search Console, YouTube, Google Ads and Sheets), Meta (Facebook Pages, Instagram and Meta Ads), LinkedIn, Pinterest, Snapchat, Amazon Ads, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Salesforce and Mailchimp. Each platform's own terms and privacy policy apply to your use of it.
